# Liber Ventorum 风起之书 — decisions

Made from a blank page. "Base" is the technique base, Visual Investigation (visual-investigation); "—" means new.

| Section | Base | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Essence | Real footage and documents from one event, synced on one clock | One era, one code-drawn object, one line; the year stamp is the clock; captions are the only voice | The channel tells 2,500 years of history with no footage, and posts every other day |
| 2 Materials | Greyscale archival footage, photos, scans | Objects drawn in code under one warm lamp; a seeded star sky; line maps marked 示意 | Ancient China has no footage; code-drawn objects are reusable across episodes |
| 3 Colour | Cold black `#0B0C0E`, signal red `#FF4A2E` | Warm black `#0E0C0A`, paper `#E8DCC0`, gold `#C9A45C` for what is read, seal red `#A8322A` for seals and one turning point | Warm, classical, readable on phones; gold and red each mean one thing |
| 4 Type | Noto Sans SC, bilingual subtitles 34 px | Noto Serif SC captions 40 px, one line, gold keywords; Cormorant SC for `LIBER VENTORUM`; IBM Plex Mono for numbers | Serif reads as history; one line suits Douyin |
| 4 Type | Timestamps on evidence | **Year stamp** top left (year · place, odometer roll) and **source card** top right (book title + red seal `风起`) | The series' signature: every screen says when, where and from which book |
| 5 Motion | 30 fps, hard cuts with white flashes | 24 fps, slow continuous moves, dark-down / light-up between shots | Calm, museum-at-night pace |
| 7 Sound | Score, foley and voice at −14 LUFS | Silent master with a silent stereo track; `EV` cues kept for later | No voice by choice; music is added on Douyin |
| 8 Native move | Freeze, circle, magnify; evidence map | **The wind**: Jupiter and Saturn meet, the old era blows into light and settles into the next era's object | The series is about air eras; the move is the title made visible |
| 8 Native move | — | Odometer year, loupe on the line (from archive-lighttable), counter + dots (from dataviz), what was left, the empty place | Reusable shots for every episode |
| 6 Layout | Evidence boards, sync grids | Top band for stamp and source, content 150–930, caption 960–1030; title card `LIBER VENTORUM` + title; end card with slogan, references, disclaimer | One fixed frame for the whole series |
